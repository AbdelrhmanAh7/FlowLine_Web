# Independent visual review — design-v2

Reviewed 2026-09-29. **Verdict: changes required; not a clean visual sign-off.** Three new P3 visual defects are recorded as **DV2-V01–V03** in [BUGS.md](../BUGS.md). The main surfaces retain the reference's dark surface hierarchy, compact controls, canvas, inspector and responsive shell. Light mode is coherent in the sampled views. Several larger layout differences need an owner decision; they are not silently treated as approved palette or motion changes. Previously reported DV2-Q01–Q05 remain separate findings, not duplicates or claimed fixes.

## Typography, geometry and colour

The compact Inter UI, semibold headings, monospace metadata/payloads, quiet one-pixel separators and 8px node rounding broadly follow slides 3–4. The sampled builder shell computed **13px / 20px, weight 400**, with the Inter/IBM Plex Sans Arabic font stack, and the desktop sidebar measured **240px**. See [geometry](geometry.json). These samples support the visual comparison; they do not prove every title, line-height or glyph fallback matches the scale.

Dark surface roles stay recognizable across screens. Light backgrounds, inputs, cards, scrims and payload panels are internally consistent and visually legible in the captured views. Primary actions remain violet; success, warning, error and information retain consistent roles. The documented darker light-theme accent and brighter dark-theme accent text are treated as the approved light/accessibility remap, not exact-hex regressions. Trigger/logic hues are close in light mode, a documented tradeoff; icons and labels still distinguish them. This review did not run a full contrast audit.

**System theme was exercised through the UI and emulated OS colour scheme:** with `data-theme=system`, light produced body background `rgb(244,244,245)` and dark `rgb(9,9,11)`. [Measurements](system-theme.json), [light](screenshots/landing-system-light-en-1440.png), [dark](screenshots/landing-system-dark-en-1440.png).

## Unclear differences requiring an owner decision

1. **U1 — landing desktop composition.** Relative to slide 5's application region, the implementation's centered header/hero are narrower and the English hero wraps differently at 1440px. Approved scroll effects explain transformation and movement, but do not by themselves approve static content width or typography/layout changes. Decide whether the current composition is intended or should move closer to the reference.
2. **U2 — gallery/catalog density.** Templates use three desktop columns rather than the reference's two, with chains wrapping/ellipsizing and additional setup/readiness sections. Integrations use taller three-column catalog cards rather than the compact reference grid. Honest setup/readiness content is justified by real functionality, but approval of the changed grid and anatomy was not established by the five visual approvals. Decide whether to retain the expanded layouts or restore the reference density while keeping honest states.
3. **U3 — settings information structure.** The deck shows members, keys and billing together alongside four navigation entries. The implementation has nine tabs and one section at a time, with usage separate from billing. Real functionality explains the extra destinations; the visual brief does not explicitly approve reorganizing the information structure. Owner decision needed before claiming reference fidelity.

These are recorded as unclear rather than invented defects or implied owner approvals. They should be resolved alongside the three confirmed defects before visual sign-off.
