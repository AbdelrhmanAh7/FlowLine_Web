## Limits

- No live SaaS connection, paid billing checkout, or configured SSO identity provider could be exercised. The SSO observation covers the local unconfigured form response only.
- No fault API or provider outage was available. Production rate limits ruled out rapid sign-up stress testing.
- The real local model is nondeterministic. The six Copilot requests above are this run's observed sample, not a guarantee of future model outputs. The receipt draft was intentionally not run to verify the no-run creation contract; the separate lead draft was run to test its generated behavior.
- This was focused exploratory browser coverage at one Chromium version and seven sampled mobile routes, not exhaustive control, browser, or human UAT coverage.
