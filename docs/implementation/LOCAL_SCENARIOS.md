# Everyday scenarios

Owner-requested expansion, 2026-09-30. The gallery now has twelve additional runnable examples alongside the original three. Every example includes editable sample input and an explanation of the saved result, in English and Arabic.

| Scenario | Useful for | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Low Stock List | Shops, cafes, small inventories | Products at or below their reorder level, ordered by shortfall |
| Invoice Follow-up List | Freelancers and small businesses | Unpaid overdue invoices, days overdue and total due |
| Quote Calculator | Service businesses and freelancers | Line totals, subtotal, optional discount and final total; no tax |
| Order Packing List | Small shops and fulfilment teams | Items needed for outstanding orders |
| Expense Category Summary | Individuals and small teams | Spending grouped by category |
| Subscription Review | Individuals and small businesses | Recurring subscriptions summarized for review |
| Weekly Task Plan | Individuals and teams | Tasks selected in priority order within a stated time budget |
| Event Attendee Summary | Event and community organizers | Registration and check-in summary with email-based deduplication |
| Registration Check | Courses, events and clubs | Valid registrations and records needing correction |
| Contact List Cleanup | Small sales and community teams | Normalized contacts and duplicate-email handling |
| Support Backlog Summary | Support teams | Open-ticket workload summary |
| Survey Score Summary | Educators, service and product teams | Summary of submitted scores |

Open **Templates**, choose an example, and use it to create your own flow. Open its first input step to edit the sample, then run the flow and inspect the saved result. The examples do not connect accounts, send messages, place orders or modify external services. They are structured-input starting points, not general spreadsheet importers.

The landing page presents six examples; onboarding presents up to three suited to the selected goal. All fifteen remain available in the gallery. The weekly plan preserves priority: after the first task exceeds the remaining budget, it and all following tasks wait. Quote amounts use one currency and two-decimal rounding; tax is excluded. Dates and review windows are explicit inputs.

Verification is recorded against the final checkpoint in `artifacts/beta-execution/20260930-landing-executor/`. Local execution evidence does not certify live integrations, provider access, cloud AI quality or Pi deployment.
